# My principles (v0, distilled 2026-10-16)

These apply across everything I do: code, translation, annotation, job search, research, design and art. Each principle notes where it came from. Add or edit only through `distill-principles`, with my approval.

1. **Study before building.** Before making anything, find out how the best others have done it and what the current state of the art is. AI works best from good examples. *(From: technical features for Resonance, research requests)*
2. **Look in the right places, at the right depth.** Every search has a defined scope, source tiers and depth, agreed upfront. *(From: web research)*
3. **Consider adjacent ideas.** Things slightly off-tangent can combine into something better. Explore them, and label them as speculative. *(From: technical research)*
4. **Check it's deployable, not just interesting.** Judge every option on maturity, fit, cost and maintenance. *(From: technical research)*
5. **Leave room to experiment.** Next to the proven option, try one new thing with a clear success metric. *(From: technical research)*
6. **State of the art, as of today.** Always use the current date and flag anything outdated. *(From: research requests)*
7. **Intent and context first.** Every task, log and decision records why it was done, the situation, and what kind of thing it is, the way data gets labeled. *(From: logging and schema discussion)*
8. **Every step has a justification and proof.** If there's no proof, it isn't verified. *(From: logging discussion)*
9. **Verify independently.** Nothing is "done" until a separate checker confirms it against criteria agreed in advance. *(From: verification discussion)*
10. **Learn from every mistake.** Log what went wrong, the root cause, how to prevent it and how to repeat what worked, then turn it into a rule after review. *(From: the computer-use mishap pattern)*
11. **Never lose context between handoffs.** Every handoff leaves pointers. Anything important lives in files, not in chat. *(From: handoffs, the laptop loss)*
12. **Nothing lives in only one place.** Back up everything, and make setup reproducible. *(From: the laptop loss)*
13. **Break things into parts.** Decompose inputs (documents, assets, tasks) into labeled parts with provenance before working on them. *(From: asset decomposition, intake)*
14. **Turn what I say into principles.** Distill how I think into reusable principles that carry over across domains, design included. *(From: this file's origin)*
15. **Ask first, then act.** Start non-trivial work with a short scope interview, and reuse past answers. *(From: interview skill)*
16. **Improve on several measures at once.** Correctness, cost, time, style and real outcomes, with changes adopted only if they don't make the test cases worse. *(From: RL discussion)*
17. **Never compromise on quality.** Aim for world-class every time: the best available method, verified, with nothing left half-done. If a constraint forces a trade-off, say so explicitly and propose how to remove it. Never settle silently. *(From: compaction/handoff discussion, "I don't want compromise ever")*
