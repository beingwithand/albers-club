Frequently asked questions

What do I need to run Resonance?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS/iPadOS 26.2 or later with Apple Intelligence. The reading and AI features rely on Apple's on-device models, which require this.

Is Resonance free?

Yes — the full reader is free: all voices, highlights, notes, vocabulary, the journal, and on-device AI. Premium adds cross-device sync, larger imports, audiobook export, customization, and deeper synthesis. New users get a 15-day free trial of Premium.

How do I restore a purchase or manage my subscription?

In the app: Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases. To cancel or change billing: device Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Where is my data? Is it private?

What you read, highlight, and note stays on your device. Only generic document text-processing is sent to a server, without your identity. See the Privacy Policy.

Why is a voice or model downloading?

High-quality on-device voices and the language model download once on first use, then run fully offline. This can be a few hundred MB — use Wi-Fi.

A book imported with messy text or wrong chapters.

Tell us the title and where it went wrong at hello@albers.club — extraction quality is something we actively improve.
