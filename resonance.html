FAQ
Is it really free?

Yes — the full reader is free: all voices, highlights, notes, journal, lexicon, and on-device AI. Premium adds sync, larger imports, audiobook export, and deeper synthesis. New users get a 15-day trial of everything.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone or iPad on iOS/iPadOS 26.2 or later with Apple Intelligence — the reading and AI features rely on Apple's on-device models.

Is my reading private?

What you read, highlight, and note stays on your device. Only generic document text-processing is sent to a server, without your identity.
